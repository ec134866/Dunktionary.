About The Dunktionary

The Dunktionary is a community-driven guide to the world of acrobatic dunking — documenting passes, dunks, and trains from across the globe. It aims to celebrate creativity and progression in tricking and dunk culture.

This site is not affiliated with or officially endorsed by any University, NBA, or Acrobatic Dunk team.
