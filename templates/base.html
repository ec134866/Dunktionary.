About The Dunktionary

The Dunktionary is a community-driven guide to the world of acrobatic dunking — documenting passes, dunks, and trains from across the globe. It aims to celebrate the creativity, history, and progression of acrobatic dunking.

This site is not affiliated with or officially endorsed by any NBA, university, or other acrobatic dunk team.
